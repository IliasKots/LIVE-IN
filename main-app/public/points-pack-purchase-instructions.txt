PLAYLIVE POINTS — PURCHASE AND DELIVERY GUIDE

Use this guide only after PlayLive announces that points checkout is available.
If checkout is unavailable, do not pay a standalone itch.io page expecting an
automatic points credit.

1. Sign in to your verified PlayLive account in the launcher.
2. Click the points +, choose a pack, then choose Buy.
3. Sign in to the SAME PlayLive account if the website asks you to do so.
4. Complete the selected points sub-product checkout on itch.io.
5. After payment, click itch.io's claim action for that purchase.
6. PlayLive verifies the purchase and credits the account that started checkout.
   Wait for the PlayLive confirmation.
7. Return to the launcher and check your balance and history. Use Check again
   if confirmation is delayed.

USD minimums and total credits (bonuses already included):
Starter   $5     5,000 points
Regular  $10    10,000 points
Pro      $25    27,500 points, including 10% bonus
Max      $50    57,500 points, including 15% bonus
Ultimate $100  120,000 points, including 20% bonus

Extra payment above the selected pack's minimum still delivers that pack once.
Paying again is a separate purchase; retrying a claim is not.

This file contains no redemption code. Downloading it or the launcher, owning
the itch.io project, closing checkout or returning to PlayLive does not credit
points. Buy the selected points sub-product through the account-linked PlayLive
flow and claim its verified purchase.

If payment was cancelled, no points are added. If login expired, sign in to
the original PlayLive account and continue the saved claim in that browser.
Once PlayLive has received and saved the verified claim, Check again can finish
delivery after restart. If you never clicked itch.io's claim action, return to
your purchase and use it. Do not pay again just because delivery is delayed.

If a claim expires or remains unavailable, contact PlayLive through the official
support channel listed on its website with your purchase reference. Do not share
passwords, session tokens or raw signed claim links. Direct itch.io purchases
without PlayLive account binding require support verification.
